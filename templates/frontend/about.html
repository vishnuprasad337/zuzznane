Meet Our Founder

A Journey Stitched with Faith and Love

Teena Juby is the Founder and CEO of Zuzanne, a premium Indian brand dedicated to creating elegant sacramental attire for Baptism, First Holy Communion, and other cherished Christian celebrations.

With a postgraduate degree in Fashion Design, Teena founded Zuzanne in 2016 with a vision to create timeless garments that beautifully honor life’s most sacred moments. Inspired by her faith in God and devotion to Mother Mary, every Zuzanne creation reflects purity, elegance, and exceptional craftsmanship.

Beyond her entrepreneurial journey, Teena is the wife of Juby Isac, Managing Director of Lunar Rubber Pvt. Ltd., a devoted mother of five daughters, and a Partner at Prathal Food Products. She believes that faith, family, and purpose are the strongest foundations for meaningful success and lasting impact.

Today, Zuzanne is trusted by families across India and around the world for its handcrafted ceremonial wear, blending tradition, premium quality, and timeless elegance.

“Every garment we create is stitched with faith, Prayerfully crafted with love, and designed to become a treasured memory for generations.”

Teena Juby
Founder & Lead Designer, Zuzanne
Since 2016

Rooted in Faith. Crafted with Grace.

Founded in 2016, Zuzanne is a unique brand dedicated to crafting premium christening outfits and ceremonial attire for babies. Inspired by Psalm 92:12–14, Zuzanne symbolizes a plant in the House of God—firmly rooted in faith, blooming fresh and flourishing in the loving presence of Mother Mary.

Every Zuzanne creation reflects the purity, grace, and sacredness of a child's first step into the Christian faith. Combining timeless craftsmanship with elegant design, we create heirloom-quality christening gowns, baptism robes, ceremonial suits, rompers, and accessories that honor this precious occasion.

Each garment is thoughtfully handcrafted using premium fabrics, delicate lace, intricate embroidery, and meticulous attention to detail, ensuring exceptional comfort and lasting beauty. From premature baby gowns to toddler christening outfits for both boys and girls, every piece is designed to become a treasured part of your family's spiritual journey.

For nearly a decade, Zuzanne has been trusted by families to make christenings and other sacred ceremonies truly memorable. More than a clothing brand, Zuzanne represents faith, tradition, and the joy of celebrating life's most meaningful moments.
